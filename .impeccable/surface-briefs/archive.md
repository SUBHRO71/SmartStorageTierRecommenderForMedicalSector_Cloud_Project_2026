## Audience and task
Archive administrator reviews study completeness, clinical linkage and observed placement before acting. Real input is uploaded permitted files; sample metadata is labeled and never supplies fake MRI pixels.

## Chosen direction
Light archive workspace, mint review queue, navy fixed navigation, warm amber attention and stable tier hues. MRI detail uses a dark image canvas. Reading order follows scope → inventory → review/placement → study rows → evidence/operations.

## First viewport
Show archive scope and provenance; immediately expose complete-study and clinical-link denominators. A mint review queue sits beside a byte-based placement bar, with the study manifest below. An empty archive presents actual import and labeled sample actions instead of invented numbers.

## Signature interaction
Navigation moves the workspace content by a small visible displacement; placement bars adjust gently as archive scope changes. Menu and filters respond promptly. Content stays visible throughout, and reduced motion disables all authored animation.

## Cross-surface reach
The same teal action, border-based panels, restrained typography, state labels and file/manifests recur across import, study inspection, quality and operations. MRI dark surfaces are confined to inspection.

## Risks and open facts
